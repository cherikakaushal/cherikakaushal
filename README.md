## `whoami`

I'm a **Computer Science & Engineering undergraduate** interested in the space where:

**software engineering × research × machine learning × data systems**

meet.

I like building things that are useful, breaking them to understand their limits, and then asking why they behaved the way they did.

My interests currently sit around:

- 🔬 **Research & experimental software**
- 🤖 **Machine learning & AI**
- 🧠 **Human-centered and intelligent systems**
- 📊 **Data-intensive applications**
- 🛠️ **Full-stack & backend engineering**
- 🧩 **Reliable and resilient systems**
- 🌐 **Web, APIs & distributed applications**
- 🥽 **Immersive / VR systems**

> **I don't just want software to work.  
> I want to understand what happens when it doesn't.**

### 🛡️ When Systems Break

An experimental project exploring **machine-learning robustness under noise and imperfect conditions**.

Instead of asking only:

> *"How accurate is the model?"*

the project asks:

> *"What happens when the assumptions around the model stop being true?"*

**Focus**

`Machine Learning` · `Robustness` · `Noise` · `Experimentation`
